About BMCCA

Bharata Mata College of Commerce and Arts

Bharata Mata College of Commerce and Arts (BMCCA) is a private, self-financing, co-educational institution established in 2014. It is located at Choondy (Chunangamvely), Aluva, Ernakulam district, Kerala, PIN 683112.

BMCCA is affiliated with Mahatma Gandhi University (MGU), Kottayam. The college prospectus states that it is owned and managed by the Archdiocese of Ernakulam–Angamaly.
